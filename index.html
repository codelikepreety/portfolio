AeroTwin — Aero Piston Engine Digital Twin (Phase 1 + Phase 2)
Phase 1: a physics-based digital twin of a MALE-UAV aero piston engine, driven by manually-entered operating parameters, with a React/Plotly dashboard and a threshold-based ("conventional, reactive") health-monitoring layer. Phase 2: a synthetic labeled dataset generated by running the Phase-1 physics twin under many fault scenarios, with three trained models layered on top — an anomaly detector, a fault classifier, and a Remaining-Useful-Life (RUL) regressor — replacing/augmenting the threshold system to predict degradation trends, estimate RUL, and compare live behavior against expected behavior.
Physics-Based Digital Twin Anomaly Detection Fault Classification RUL Regression React/Plotly Dashboard
